## What it found

- **Two-column PDF (`report`).** PdfPig's table reconstruction reads the two columns as one three-column table: no
  heading survives, and the title's lines interleave with the first paragraph's. With `ReconstructTables=false` the
  table goes, but the lines of the two columns interleave instead and text similarity falls to 0.436. Neither path
  orders text by layout.
- **A table across a page break (`pricelist`).** The rows on page 2 come out as a second table without the header,
  shifted one column to the right, with one wrapped cell split over two rows and the footnote below the table pulled
  in as a cell. Nearly every cell is there (0.989), but only 42% of the data cells are still under their column header.
- **Merged cells (`invoice`).** `消費税（10%）`, spanning the first four columns, is placed in the second column.
- **Running headers and page numbers on scans (`contract-scan`).** The vision model transcribes them with the body on
  both pages; on the digital twin PdfPig removes all of them.
- **Sheet labels (`ledger`).** ElBruno writes each sheet as `## Sheet: <name>`, text the workbook does not contain.

Not measured: a paragraph split at a page break (`contract`, article 6) or two paragraphs run together (`invoice`, the
payee and the due date). The block checks ignore whitespace and cannot see either.
